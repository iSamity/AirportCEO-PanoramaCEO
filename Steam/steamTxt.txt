[h3]Note[/h3]
This mod requires the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3109136766]AirportCEO Mod Loader[/url] to function. Please make sure to have it correctly downloaded and installed!

[h3]Description[/h3]
A code mod that extends the camera zoom range and lets you adjust pan speed. Defaults match the normal game—change Zoom Min/Max and Pan Speed in the configuration menu (F1) if you want.

[h3]Features[/h3]
- Configurable camera zoom range (min and max)
- Adjustable camera pan speed multiplier
